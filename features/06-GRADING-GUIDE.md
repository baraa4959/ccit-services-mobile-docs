# Grading guide — دليل التقديرات

[← Home](../README.md) · [Reference API](../api/grading-guide/README.md)

This is a **read-only public policy reference for signed-in students**, not an academic transcript or personal gradebook. `GET /services/grading` returns official University of Bisha grade bands, their Arabic labels and 5/4-point weights, special codes with GPA and pass flags, and minimum GPA thresholds for graduation labels, with source metadata. Render this response directly; do not calculate or invent a student's course grades. Reference cited by current website: third edition of the study and examinations regulation (1445 AH), Annex 1 p.50; overall rating Article 37. Provide table plus separate special/outcome and graduation categories, accessible RTL headings.
